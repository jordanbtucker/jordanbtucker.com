Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

This policy covers text messages sent by Jordan Tucker for Iris, a personal chat app.

What we collect

We collect the mobile phone number that opts in to Iris text notifications, along with the record of that opt-in and of any opt-out.

How we use it

We use the phone number only to send Iris notifications to that number and to honor opt-out requests.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Messages are delivered through our SMS provider, Twilio, which processes the phone number only to deliver them.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jordanbtucker@gmail.com.
